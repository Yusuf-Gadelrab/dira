# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DIRA is a small, solo-maintained security tool. The bar for participating — filing an issue,
opening a PR, commenting on a discussion — is the same one that applies to any technical
community:

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific. "This is wrong" is not actionable; "this rule false-positives on `x.py:12` because
  `y`" is.
- Assume good faith, especially in a security tool where a reported false positive or false
  negative is the most valuable thing anyone can give the project.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or unwelcome sexual attention, in
  issues, PRs, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Email **yusuf.gadelrab06@gmail.com** with `DIRA CONDUCT` in the subject. Reports are handled
directly by the maintainer and kept confidential. Expect an acknowledgement within 72 hours.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block a user for behavior that violates
this standard. There is no larger moderation team — decisions are made by one person, in good
faith, and can be appealed by replying to the same email.

## Scope

This applies to the GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions) and any official DIRA channel
(the project's own social posts and their reply threads). It does not extend to unrelated
platforms or personal accounts.
